# Stardust: Celestial Bodies

The bodies you harvest. Each body has a spawn weight. When a body spawns, the game makes a random roll weighted by spawn weight, so a higher weight appears more often. The weights below are placeholders.

| Body | Spawn weight | Made of | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asteroid | 60 | 2–5 random elements, covered in regolith, water ice, or basalt | The smallest body |
| Moon | 25 | Same as an asteroid: 2–5 random elements, covered in regolith, water ice, or basalt | Massively bigger than an asteroid |
| Planet | 10 | A wide range of element and natural material deposits | |
| Star | 5 | All elements, in varying amounts | Insanely hot to approach |

"Ice moons" and "volcanic moons" in other docs mean moons covered in water ice or basalt.
